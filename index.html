Welcome

A sanctuary made for slow living

Set between the forest and the shore, Eterrno is a small resort for people who want to switch off. Every villa looks out to the water, every meal is made with local ingredients, and every day moves at your own pace.

Our team takes care of the details so you can spend your time on what matters: long swims, good food and real rest.
